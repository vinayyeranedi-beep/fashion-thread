Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum order size? ↕
There isn't one! We print single custom tees for personal projects as well as batches of several thousand for corporate and college runs.
How long does printing take? ↕
Standard orders leave the shop within 72 hours. Larger bulk orders are scheduled based on your event date.
Can I see a sample before bulk printing? ↕
Yes! For bulk and corporate orders, we print a sample piece first so you can inspect color, fit, and print quality before the full batch runs.
Do you deliver outside Vizianagaram? ↕
Yes, we ship across Andhra Pradesh and all over India with express tracking.
